A singular career

Nick Hanauer is an entrepreneur, investor, and civic leader whose work spans technology, public policy, and economic reform. He was the first non-family investor in Amazon and co-founded aQuantive, which Microsoft acquired for $6.4 billion.

Today, Hanauer is the founder of Civic Ventures and a leading advocate for middle-out economics. Through public policy, writing, podcasts, and widely viewed talks, he challenges trickle-down orthodoxy and argues that prosperous economies are built by thriving middle classes.
